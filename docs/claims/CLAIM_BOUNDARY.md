# Claim Boundary

## Permitted

- read-level methylation heterogeneity under the declared M1 contract;
- local methyl-group/sSNV co-occurrence under declared exact-test and FDR contracts;
- molecule-pattern-compatible mutation-state families;
- objective-certified, family-complete or explicitly incomplete topology states.

## Not permitted

- confirmed biological clone or clone count;
- unique ancestor, parent, time order or cellular lineage;
- partner sSNV as orthogonal somatic truth;
- causal or independent-marker claims from 20 bp spacing;
- absence of confounding when an M2 axis is underpowered or indeterminate;
- winner selection from capped, deadline or incomplete families.

## Model labels

- Evo-M0 recurrence allowed: primary.
- Evo-M1 strict infinite-sites: sensitivity only after allele-specific CN/LOH gate.
- Evo-M2 Dollo loss-supported: `UNRESOLVED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` in v1.
