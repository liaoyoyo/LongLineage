FROZEN METHOD CONTRACT
1. Raw evidence codes are R, A, O and X only. R/A require a usable BQ.
   O is a measured non-REF/non-ALT base and X is unavailable; both are
   marginalized to X for structural and likelihood projections.
2. The conditional biallelic emission uses e=10^(-BQ/10), clamps e to
   [1e-6,0.25], and emits match=(1-e)/(1-2e/3) and
   flip=(e/3)/(1-2e/3).
3. Structural patterns are consolidated R/A/X groups at the configured
   minimum multiplicity. Active loci come only from retained structural ALT
   calls. All informative BQ groups, including groups below that structural
   threshold, remain in abundance scoring.
4. Each distinct complete-family vertex set is evaluated exactly once.
   Parent choices and edge scores are absent from the ranking API.
5. Mixture weights are optimized on the simplex by deterministic pairwise
   exact line search. max(gradient)-N is the concave global log-likelihood gap
   certificate. Any non-finite or uncertified candidate suppresses rank.
6. The complete published tie class conservatively retains every candidate
   whose certified upper bound is within the frozen tie tolerance of the best
   lower bound.
7. Family cap and family deadline publish zero candidates and no rank.
   Objective abstention and numerical-certificate failure also publish no rank.
8. Parent edges are evaluated only after a top-ranked fixed node set exists.
   Exact parent tie counts use arbitrary-precision decimal integers. A mapping
   is published only when every child has one best parent.
9. A no-read unary hidden chain is projection-only: a candidate-bound
   multi-mutation edge, exact factorial order count and
   UNRESOLVED_NO_READ_EVIDENCE state. It cannot change objective, family or
   abundance rank.
10. Evo-M0 recurrence-allowed remains primary. Strict infinite-sites is
    NOT_EVALUATED sensitivity-only, and loss-supported Dollo remains unresolved.
