DOMAIN SEPARATION
membership.joint_complete_readset_sha256 binds the P4 M1-core reads that are
R/A complete at every selected partner. The P5 adapter receives no M1 core
assignment and therefore does not claim to recompute or validate that digest.
complete_ra_projection_readset_sha256 instead describes every touching unique
projection that is R/A complete at every membership locus. The two domains may
legitimately differ. The adapter evidence digest binds both domains without
equating them.

MEMBERSHIP AND ADAPTER INVARIANTS
- Every cooccurrence site may enter the candidate funnel, including an M1-
  unstable or M2-ineligible site.
- Joint PASS alone carries one to three selected partners in P4 ranking order
  plus a lowercase P4 complete-core-readset SHA. FAIL/NOT_EVALUATED require
  partners and digest to be absent.
- Funnel precedence is M2 ineligible, then joint FAIL/NOT_EVALUATED. Only
  M2-eligible + joint PASS creates a unit.
- Zero instantiated units is a valid OK_EMPTY plan with conserved funnel
  counts and a plan digest. No synthetic solver-abstain unit is inserted.
- Units sort by dataset_order, canonical chr1..chr22, focal_site_order and
  site_order-canonical loci before dense zero-based unit_order assignment.
- Partners are in the focal inclusive +/-5000 bp window, pairwise separated
  by at least 20 bp, unique, same-dataset and same-contig.
- The adapter includes each unique read projection touching at least one locus,
  preserves R/A/O/X and partial subcubes, sets multiplicity to one, and orders
  output by opaque read ID.
- R/A requires BQ >=20 and BQ !=255. O/X is emitted with null BQ. An input X
  carrying BQ is malformed; O may carry measured BQ upstream but is explicitly
  marginalized to null for topology likelihood.
- RG occurrence counts never weight topology observations.
- HP/PS is not a topology membership partition and is not read by the adapter.
- CN/LOH is not asserted by membership. Absent authority remains
  NOT_EVALUATED/UNRESOLVED in the P5 serializer.

NEGATIVE AND INVARIANCE COVERAGE
- unsupported schema version and noncanonical contig
- zero/four selected partners
- focal distance 5001 rejected; distance 5000 accepted
- partner spacing 19 rejected; spacing 20 accepted
- cross-dataset and cross-contig partner
- duplicate partner, duplicate/conflicting focal and conflicting locus binding
- PASS missing partners or SHA
- FAIL/NOT_EVALUATED carrying forbidden selection evidence
- missing marker, projected call, R/A BQ, BQ<20 and BQ=255
- X carrying BQ
- duplicate opaque read ID or duplicate read projection
- P4/P5 complete-readset canonical byte differential
- all-rejected zero-unit funnel conservation
- candidate/read/marker completion order, locale and HP/PS invariance
- O-to-null-BQ, retained X/O partial subcube and solver evidence-builder READY
- multiplicity=1 and R/A/O/X/read-count conservation
